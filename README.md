# Thesis-Scanning
This includes the scanning codes for master thesis.
# Overview
As part of my graduate degree thesis for M.Eng(Architecture and Urban Design) degree at the University of Tokyo, I developed a scanning system that uses hand-held controller to scan the point position of building materials. The HTC-Vive hand-held controller, which is normally used for gaming and VR application, is reprogrammed to be able to scan the exact point of the material and the scanned data is sent online to a google-sheet. This scanning system is developed so that workers working remotely can scan the built-structure without needing to know any technical skills except pressing a button on the controller. This scanned points are then downloaded by the structural design team who is working in another remote location. The scanned points are used to check the current status of the structure and then after necessary calculations, a new design is generated based on the result. This new design is sent to the workers, and they assemble a new layer of materials using a Guiding system. After another layer is assembled, the scanning is done again on the new layer and the feedback loop is continued until the structure is finished.
